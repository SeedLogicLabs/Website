Most companies start with a product. We started with a habit: noticing the small,
stubborn frictions in everyday life and asking why nobody had fixed them properly for
people like us.

Tracking money across mobile wallets, bank apps and cash felt like bookkeeping. Opening
an account meant photocopying an ID and waiting. And the kids around us could name a
hundred anime characters but had never been handed a tool to make one of their own.

Each of those observations became a prototype. The prototypes became **SeedLogic Labs**.

## Engineered for growth

Our tagline is a promise about what software should do for the person holding the phone.
Growth means understanding your money a little better each month. It means proving who
you are without friction. It means making something you are proud of, and reaching a
government service without a queue.

We are engineers first, so "engineered" is doing real work in that sentence too. We care
about the unglamorous parts: data minimisation, offline behaviour, honest status labels,
and shipping small.

## What we are building first

- **[PesaPath](/products/pesapath)** is a personal finance app for budgets, income and
  expense tracking, goals and plain-language insights. In development.
- **[ID Scanner SDK](/products/id-scanner-sdk)** lets businesses scan national IDs,
  passports and driver licences with liveness detection, straight from their own app.
  In development, with a developer preview on the product page.
- **[Creature Codex](/products/creature-codex)** turns fans into makers: design
  creatures, build a codex, tell stories. In development.
- **[Online Cyber](/products/online-cyber)** is our research project into bringing
  cyber-cafe and government services to the phone. Research phase.
